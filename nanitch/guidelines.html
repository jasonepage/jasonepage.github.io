Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Nanitch works because people are decent to each other. These are the rules that keep it a warm, safe, non-sexual place to discover spots and meet people. Break them and you're out — it's that simple.

The short version

Be kind. Keep it non-sexual. Respect the land, the law, and each other's privacy. Tell the truth about spots. That's basically it.

The rules

1. Keep it non-sexual

Naturism here is about being comfortable in your skin, not hooking up. No sexual content, no solicitation, no treating anyone like a target. This is the whole point of Nanitch.

2. No nudity in the app

Nudity belongs at the spot, not on your screen. No nude photos in profiles, spots, or anywhere in the app. All photos are safe-for-work and moderated.

3. Consent always

Ask before photographing anyone. Never share a photo of someone without their okay. Don't reveal who was somewhere, and never "out" anyone as a naturist.

4. Respect the spot and the land

Leave No Trace: pack out everything, stay on trails, park legally. Don't post spots on private property or anywhere nudity isn't legal or tolerated. Fragile places get loved to death — be thoughtful about what you make public.

5. Tell the truth

Add accurate spots and honest confirmations. Don't post misleading, unsafe, or fake information. If a spot changed or closed, say so.

6. Be welcoming

Everyone was a first-timer once. Every age, shape, and body is welcome. No harassment, hate, or discrimination — full stop.

7. Follow the law

You're responsible for knowing and following local nudity, trespassing, and park rules. Nanitch surfaces what we know, but the final call is yours.

Reporting and consequences

See something off? Use the report button anywhere in the app — a real person reviews reports, typically within 24 hours. Depending on severity, we may remove content, warn, suspend, or permanently ban. Serious violations (sexual content involving the app, harassment, anything involving minors) are removed immediately and may be reported to authorities.

Questions or appeals: hello@nanitch.com.
