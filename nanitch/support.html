Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Me → Delete account. It permanently removes your account and all your data. This can't be undone.

How do I report a spot, person, or content?

Tap the report button on the item (spots have one on their detail page; long-press activity in the feed). A moderator reviews reports, typically within 24 hours.

How does the map decide what's trustworthy?

Official spots are curated by our team. Community-submitted spots earn trust as real members confirm them (New → Trusted → Popular), and moderators can mark standout ones Verified.

What's a "private pin"?

A spot you save just for yourself — it appears only on your map. You can submit it publicly later if you want to share it, and it'll go through moderation first.

Is Nanitch sexual or a dating app?

No. It's a non-sexual naturism and nature community. There are no DMs, and nudity isn't allowed in the app. See our Community Guidelines.

Is it legal to visit these spots?

It depends on the spot, which is why every listing shows its legal status. You're responsible for confirming legality and safety before you go. See our Terms.
