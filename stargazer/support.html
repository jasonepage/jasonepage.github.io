Sokobop — Support

How can we help?

Sokobop is a push-box puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Here are answers to common questions. For anything else, email jasonepage@gmail.com.

How do I play?

Move Bop into a coral box to push it onto a teal goal. You can push but never pull. If a box reaches a corner where it cannot move, use Undo. Put every box on a goal to finish the room.

How do I move Bop?

Swipe anywhere, tap beside Bop, use the arrow or WASD keys, or connect a game controller. Undo uses the on-screen button or Z/U on a keyboard.

What do the three stamps mean?

Finishing always earns one stamp. Efficient solves earn two, and matching the solver's best move count earns all three. You can replay any room to improve your result.

How do rooms unlock?

Finish the available rooms to open the next part of the campaign. Sokobop has no subscription and no ads.

Is it really offline? Do I need an account?

The campaign works offline and needs no account. Community levels require an internet connection. There are no ads or tracking.

Where is my progress saved?

Your campaign progress, stamps, streak and settings are stored on your device. There is no required account or cloud sync, so local progress is removed if you delete the app.

How do I change sounds, haptics, theme, or motion?

Open Settings to toggle Sound, Music and Haptics, choose 2D or 3D play, and select a Bop skin. Sokobop respects iOS Reduce Motion and supports VoiceOver.

How do I turn on the daily reminder?

In Settings, enable "Daily reminder" and choose a time. It only works if you grant notification permission, and you can turn it off anytime.

How do I share a video of my solve?

After finishing a room, use the share option to create a short replay. Nothing is posted automatically by Sokobop.

How do community levels work?

Browse and play levels made by other players, or create one in the level maker. Community features require an internet connection; campaign rooms do not.
