Stargazer: Daily Star Puzzles — Support

How can we help?

Stargazer is a cozy, fully offline puzzle game. Here are answers to common questions. For anything else, email jasonepage@gmail.com and you'll get a reply.

How does the Daily Sky work?

Every day there is one new puzzle, the same for everyone, with exactly one solution. Each star shows how many lines it needs. Draw lines between stars so that every star has its number, no lines cross, and all the stars join into a single constellation. Puzzles are gentle on Monday and grow larger through the week. Luna's first lesson teaches it by doing, and you can replay the lesson from Settings.

How do I play Fallen Stars?

Swipe anywhere to walk Luna. Walk into a fallen star to push it. You can push but never pull, so a star shoved into a corner is stuck, and Undo takes back your last step. Each level gives up to three moons based on how few moves you used. There are 36 levels in three worlds. A hardware keyboard or game controller works here too.

What is the Scrapbook?

The Scrapbook holds the 19 constellations you can collect, arranged in four seasonal skies. Each Fallen Stars level brings one constellation home. Finishing a season unlocks a new color theme, earning three moons on a constellation's level turns its card gold, and solving a full week of Daily Skies unlocks the Moonlit theme.

How do worlds unlock?

The Daily Sky and the first Fallen Stars world are free. Worlds 2 and 3 are part of Full Sky, a single one-time purchase handled by Apple, with no subscription and no ads. Within Full Sky, a world opens when you finish the one before it or complete its season in the Scrapbook.

Is it really offline? Do I need an account?

Yes. Stargazer is 100% offline and needs no account or internet connection. There are no ads and no tracking.

Where is my progress saved?

Your streak, moons, Scrapbook, and settings are stored on your device. There is no online account or cloud sync, so they live on that device and are removed if you delete the app.

How do I change sounds, haptics, theme, or motion?

Open Settings (the gear, top right) to toggle Sound, Music, and Haptics independently and to pick a theme. Stargazer respects iOS Reduce Motion and supports VoiceOver.

How do I turn on the daily reminder?

In Settings, enable "Daily reminder" and choose a time. It only works if you grant notification permission, and you can turn it off anytime.

How do I share a video of my solve?

After you finish a Fallen Stars level, tap "Make a video for TikTok." The app builds a short vertical clip of your solve with sound. Tap "Save to Photos," then open TikTok, Instagram or Messages and pick it from your camera roll. Nothing is uploaded by Stargazer itself.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you bought Full Sky and it is not showing on a new device, open Settings and tap "Restore purchases." You must be signed in to the same Apple account you bought it with.
